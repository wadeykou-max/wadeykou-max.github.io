I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the [Institute of Modern Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IMP, CAS)](http://english.imp.cas.cn/).

My research focuses on **hadron and nuclear structure in Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD)**, with particular interests in nucleon structure, small-\(x\) physics, quantum information, and physics-informed machine learning. I am also interested in theoretical studies of muon-catalyzed fusion.

## 🔬 Research

My research centers on the structure and dynamics of strongly interacting systems. I combine conventional theoretical and phenomenological approaches with concepts from quantum information and modern computational methods, with close connections to observables accessible at high-energy and nuclear-physics facilities.

### Hadron Structure and QCD Phenomenology

I study the internal structure of hadrons, particularly the proton and light mesons. My work includes **nucleon structure, proton mass decomposition, gluonic and mechanical properties, parton distribution functions, small-\(x\) QCD, and diffractive processes**.

I am especially interested in extracting information on hadron structure from deep-inelastic scattering and exclusive processes, and in developing phenomenological descriptions based on QCD evolution and holographic approaches.

### Quantum Information in QCD and Nuclear Physics

I explore the role of **quantum entanglement, entropy, and information-theoretic concepts** in strongly interacting systems.

These studies include applications to small-\(x\) deep-inelastic scattering, nuclear short-range correlations, hadron structure, and nonperturbative QCD dynamics.

### Physics-Informed Machine Learning

I develop and apply **physics-informed and physics-guided machine-learning methods** to problems in strong-interaction physics.

My work in this direction includes neural-network studies of proton structure, QCD evolution, quark-antiquark color flux tubes, and confinement-related dynamics, with an emphasis on incorporating physical constraints and dynamical equations directly into machine-learning frameworks.

### Muon-Catalyzed Fusion

I am also interested in the theoretical description of **muon-catalyzed fusion (μCF)**, including microscopic reaction kinetics, muon sticking and recycling, catalytic-cycle dynamics, and energy-balance conditions.
