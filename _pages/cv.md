## 🧭 Research Profile

My research focuses on the structure and dynamics of strongly interacting systems within **Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD)**. I am particularly interested in hadron and nucleon structure, small-\(x\) physics, parton distributions, proton mass and mechanical structure, and diffractive processes.

I also explore the application of **quantum information concepts**, including quantum entanglement and entropy, to QCD and nuclear systems. Another major direction of my work is the development of **physics-informed and physics-guided machine-learning approaches** for nonperturbative QCD, hadron structure, QCD evolution, and confinement-related phenomena.

More recently, I have also been working on theoretical aspects of **muon-catalyzed fusion (μCF)**, including catalytic-cycle kinetics, muon sticking and recycling, and energy-balance conditions.
