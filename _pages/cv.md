## Research Profile

My research focuses on the structure and dynamics of strongly interacting systems within **Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD)**.

My main interests include **hadron and nucleon structure, small-\(x\) physics, parton distributions, proton mass and mechanical properties, and diffractive processes**.

I also study the application of **quantum information concepts**, including quantum entanglement and entropy, to QCD and nuclear systems, and develop **physics-informed and physics-guided machine-learning approaches** for nonperturbative strong-interaction physics.

My current research also includes theoretical studies of **muon-catalyzed fusion (μCF)**, with emphasis on catalytic-cycle kinetics, muon sticking and recycling, and energy-balance conditions.
