I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the [Institute of Modern Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IMP, CAS)](http://english.imp.cas.cn/). I received my **Ph.D. in Particle Physics and Nuclear Physics** from IMP, CAS in June 2025.

My research focuses on the **structure and dynamics of strongly interacting systems**, with particular emphasis on hadron and nuclear structure within Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD). I am also interested in connecting conventional theoretical approaches with concepts from **quantum information** and modern **physics-informed machine learning**.

---

## 🔬 Research

### Hadron Structure and QCD Phenomenology

My primary research interest is the internal structure of hadrons, especially the proton and light mesons. I study **nucleon structure, proton mass decomposition, gluonic and mechanical properties, parton distributions, small-\(x\) QCD, and diffractive processes**.

A major part of my work concerns extracting hadron-structure information from deep-inelastic scattering and exclusive vector-meson production, together with phenomenological studies based on QCD evolution and holographic QCD.

### Quantum Information in QCD and Nuclear Physics

I explore how concepts from **quantum information theory**, particularly quantum entanglement and entropy, can provide new perspectives on strongly interacting systems.

My work in this direction includes studies of **entanglement in small-\(x\) deep-inelastic scattering, nuclear short-range correlations, color dynamics, and chiral-symmetry-related phenomena**.

### Physics-Informed Machine Learning for Strong Interactions

I develop and apply **physics-informed and physics-guided machine-learning methods** to problems in strong-interaction physics.

These studies include neural-network descriptions of **quark-antiquark color flux tubes, confinement-related dynamics, proton structure, and QCD evolution**, with particular interest in incorporating theoretical constraints and dynamical equations directly into machine-learning frameworks.

### Muon-Catalyzed Fusion

I am also interested in theoretical aspects of **muon-catalyzed fusion (\(\mu\)CF)**, particularly the relation between microscopic reaction kinetics, muon sticking and recycling, catalytic cycling, and energy-balance conditions.
