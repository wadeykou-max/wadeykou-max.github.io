## Research

My research centers on the structure and dynamics of strongly interacting systems. I combine theoretical and phenomenological approaches with concepts from quantum information and modern computational methods, with particular attention to observables accessible at high-energy and nuclear-physics facilities.

### Hadron Structure and QCD Phenomenology

I study the internal structure of hadrons, particularly the proton and light mesons, with emphasis on **nucleon structure, proton mass and mechanical properties, parton distributions, small-\(x\) QCD, and diffractive processes**.

I am especially interested in extracting information on hadron structure from deep-inelastic scattering and exclusive processes, as well as in developing phenomenological descriptions based on QCD evolution and holographic approaches.

### Quantum Information in QCD and Nuclear Physics

I explore how concepts from **quantum information theory**, including quantum entanglement and entropy, can provide new perspectives on strongly interacting systems.

My work in this direction includes applications to small-\(x\) deep-inelastic scattering, nuclear short-range correlations, hadron structure, and nonperturbative QCD dynamics.

### Physics-Informed Machine Learning

I develop and apply **physics-informed and physics-guided machine-learning approaches** to strong-interaction physics.

These studies include proton structure, QCD evolution, quark-antiquark color flux tubes, and confinement-related dynamics, with an emphasis on incorporating physical constraints and dynamical equations directly into machine-learning frameworks.

### Other Current Interests

I am also interested in theoretical aspects of **muon-catalyzed fusion (μCF)**, particularly microscopic reaction kinetics, muon sticking and recycling, catalytic-cycle dynamics, and energy-balance conditions.
